Privacy Policy

Ethan's Utilities 2.0 · How we collect, store, and protect your data.

📌 What Data We Collect

When you use Ethan's Utilities, the following information may be stored:

  • Your Discord User ID — used to track balances, TOS acceptance, and blacklist status
  • Message counts within the designated raffle channel
  • Economy balances, inventory, and transaction history
  • Raffle and giveaway participation records
  • Robux deposit and withdrawal records
  • Season Pass progress and reward claim history

🔐 How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used solely to operate the bot's features:

  • Running the economy system (balances, shops, gambling, stocks)
  • Managing raffles, giveaways, drops, and season passes
  • Enforcing community rules (blacklists, warnings, TOS)
  • Processing Robux deposits and withdrawal requests
  • Spam detection and fair-play enforcement

🚫 What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell or share your data with any third parties
  • We do not store message content beyond what is required for spam detection
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • We do not access data from other servers or bots

🗑️ Data Deletion

To request removal of your data, contact staff in the Ethan's World Discord server. Note that certain records (e.g. transaction history, blacklist entries) may be retained for moderation purposes.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Ethan's Utilities is a community-run Discord bot and is not affiliated with Discord Inc. or Roblox Corporation. Use of this bot is subject to our Terms of Service and Discord's Terms of Service.

Your privacy matters.
We only store the minimum data required to run the bot. If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to staff in our Discord server.
