A Final Statement

If you're reading this, then this is likely one of the last things I'll be posting before leaving Discord.

Over the past two years, I've spent a huge amount of time on this platform. I made friends, worked on projects, helped run communities, and met people I genuinely cared about. Some of those people became what I considered my closest friends.

That's why what happened recently hurts as much as it does.

I was betrayed by people I trusted for nearly two years. People I spoke to every day. People I defended, supported, and worked alongside. Despite everything that happened, I still feel like I never received a real explanation for why this entire situation started or why it escalated as far as it did.

There have been many accusations made against me. Some have been exaggerated, some have been taken out of context, and some simply do not reflect who I am today. Regardless, I was never given a fair chance to explain my side before people started making up their minds.

One thing I have thought about a lot is whether part of this was influenced by how some people viewed me after I opened up about being a femboy. I cannot say for certain that this was the reason, but I noticed that attitudes toward me seemed to change afterward. Whether that played a role or not is something only those involved can answer.

What I can say with certainty is that personal photos of me were shared without my permission. Those photos were personal images of me as a minor. Regardless of any disagreements, arguments, or accusations, sharing someone's personal images without their consent is completely unacceptable.

Since all of this happened, I have received harassment, threats, and countless hostile messages. What began as a conflict between people turned into something much larger than it ever should have been.

I am not posting this to start drama, attack anyone, or seek sympathy. This page exists simply because I want my side of the story to be heard before I leave.

To everyone who supported me, talked with me, played games with me, worked on projects with me, or simply made my days a little better: thank you. Despite how things ended, I genuinely appreciate the good memories and the time we shared.

To the people who stood by me during everything that happened, thank you. Your support meant more than you probably realize.

And to everyone else, I wish you the best moving forward.

It's been nice knowing you all.

This marks the end of my time on Discord.

Goodbye.
