My Statement

If you're reading this, then this is likely one of the last things I will post before leaving Discord.

Over the last two years, I spent a huge amount of time on this platform. I made friends, worked on projects, helped run communities, and met people who I genuinely cared about. Some of those people became what I considered my closest friends.

That's why everything that happened recently hurts as much as it does.

I was betrayed by people I trusted for nearly two years. These were people I talked to every day, worked with, and supported. Despite everything that happened, I still feel like I never received a clear explanation for why this situation started or why it escalated as far as it did.

There have been many accusations made against me. Some have been exaggerated, some have been taken out of context, and some simply do not reflect who I am today. I was never given what felt like a fair opportunity to explain my side before people began making up their minds.

One thing I have thought about a lot is whether attitudes toward me changed after I opened up about being a femboy. I cannot say for certain that this was the reason for what happened, but I did notice that some people's behavior toward me seemed to change afterward. Whether that played a role or not is something only those involved truly know.

What I can say with certainty is that personal photos of me were shared without my permission. Those photos were personal images of me as a minor. Regardless of any disagreements, arguments, or accusations, sharing someone's personal images without their consent crosses a line that should never be crossed.

Since these events occurred, I have received harassment, threats, and hostile messages. What started as a disagreement quickly became something much larger than it ever should have been.

I am not posting this statement to start drama, attack anyone, or seek sympathy. I simply want my side of the story to exist somewhere before I leave.

Thank You

Despite how things ended, I genuinely appreciate the good memories and the time we shared together.
