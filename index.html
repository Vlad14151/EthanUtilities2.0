📋 Server Bot Guidelines
✅ Please read the bot guidelines below carefully. Violations may result in warnings, temporary blacklist, or permanent ban.
#1
Rule Changes & Agreement
These rules may be updated at any time without prior notice. By continuing to use the bot, you agree to all current and future rules.
#2
Exploits, Abuse & Economy Manipulation
Exploiting bugs, glitches, duplication, unintended command behavior, or any form of system abuse is strictly prohibited and will result in a permanent bot ban.

Attempting to gain an unfair advantage, manipulate the economy, or transfer items/currency to avoid punishment may result in asset removal, rollback, or a permanent bot ban.
#3
Automation
Using macros, self-bots, scripts, or any form of automation to interact with the bot is strictly prohibited.
#4
Bypassing Punishments
Attempting to bypass blacklists, bans, or punishments using alternate accounts or other methods will result in a permanent ban across all accounts.
#5
Bot Disruption
Intentionally lagging, crashing, spamming, or disrupting bot performance or stability is not allowed.
#6
Command Abuse & Spam
Spamming commands, abusing bot features, or overloading the bot with excessive usage is not allowed.
#7
Economy Enforcement
Staff may remove, rollback, or adjust any unfair, bugged, or exploited transactions at any time. All decisions are final.
#8
Currency Transfers
Transferring currency or items between accounts for the purpose of evading punishments, hoarding, or manipulating the economy is not allowed and may result in asset removal and a permanent ban.
#9
Season Pass & Limited Events
Season Pass progress, rewards, and event-exclusive items are tied to their respective seasons. No compensation will be given for missed rewards due to inactivity or failure to claim before the season ends.
#10
Gambling Acknowledgement
By using any gambling-related commands, you acknowledge that all outcomes are random and for entertainment purposes only. No refunds will be issued for gambling losses under any circumstances.
#11
Rate Limits
Commands may have hidden or visible rate limits to maintain bot stability. Attempting to bypass, abuse, or exploit rate limits is strictly prohibited and may result in a temporary or permanent blacklist.
#12
Code Redemption
Codes are for personal use only. Selling, distributing, or sharing codes obtained through giveaways, staff, or rewards without permission is strictly prohibited.
#13
Raffle & Giveaway Conduct
Attempting to manipulate raffle or giveaway outcomes, using multiple accounts, or using automation to gain unfair entries is strictly prohibited and will result in disqualification and a permanent ban.
#14
Robux Deposits & Withdrawals
  • Robux deposited into the system cannot be withdrawn or reversed once confirmed
  • Deposited Robux does not transfer ownership or entitle the depositor to compensation
  • Minimum 50 Robux is required to request a withdrawal
#15
Bot Dependency & Liability
The bot is provided as-is with no guarantee of uptime, availability, or continuity of features. Ethan's World is not responsible for any loss of progress, currency, items, or data due to outages, updates, crashes, or technical issues.
#16
Staff Impersonation
Impersonating staff members, the bot, or any official server entity to deceive users or gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.
#17
Refunds & Data
No refunds will be given unless valid proof is provided and approved by staff. This applies to all currencies, items, and systems. Data loss caused by errors, crashes, updates, or resets is not the bot's responsibility. All refund, restore, or compensation decisions are final and determined by staff.
#18
Warnings & Enforcement
4 warnings = temporary blacklist.
#19
Resets & Progress
The bot may reset economy, progress, or data at any time. No compensation is guaranteed unless officially announced.
✅ The final verdict lies with Ethan's World staff at all times.
Server staff will use their own judgement when enforcing our rules. Not every rule is listed — use common sense at all times. Rules may be updated at any time, and continued use of the server means you agree to follow all guidelines.
🔒 Privacy Policy
Ethan's World Utilities 2.0 · How we handle your data.

📌 What Data We Collect

When you use Ethan's World Utilities, we may store the following:

  • Your Discord User ID (to track economy, TOS acceptance, and blacklist status)
  • Message counts within the designated raffle channel
  • Economy balances, inventory, and transaction history
  • Raffle and giveaway participation records
  • Robux deposit and withdrawal records

🔐 How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used solely to operate the bot's features:

  • Running the economy system (balances, shops, gambling)
  • Managing raffles, giveaways, and season passes
  • Enforcing community rules (blacklists, warnings)
  • Processing Robux deposits and withdrawal requests

🚫 What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties
  • We do not store message content beyond what is required for spam detection
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information

🗑️ Data Deletion

If you would like your data removed, please contact staff in the Ethan's World Discord server. Note that some records (e.g. transaction history) may be retained for moderation purposes.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Ethan's World Utilities is a community bot and is not affiliated with Discord Inc. or Roblox Corporation. Use of this bot is subject to both our Terms of Service and Discord's Terms of Service.

🔒 Your privacy matters to us.
We only store what is necessary to run the bot. If you have any concerns, reach out to staff in our Discord server.
